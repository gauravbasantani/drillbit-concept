# Drillbit concept

An unofficial design concept by Gaurav Basantani: check one line before Mason's quote goes out.

A made-up painting company reviews a quote that Drillbit's AI, Mason, drafted from a phone call. On a noisy stretch of the call, "fifteen by twelve" came through as "fifty by twelve". Mason flags that one line, and the owner fixes it with one tap before the homeowner sees the price.

- Live page: https://gauravbasantani.github.io/drillbit-concept/
- Walkthrough (35 seconds): https://gauravbasantani.github.io/drillbit-concept/walkthrough.mp4

The business, homeowner, call and prices are made up. Not affiliated with Drillbit.
